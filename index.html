Our Durgotsav
More than a festival.
A community tradition.

Botnia Durgotsav, presented by Kollol Puja Samiti for the Gulshan Botnia community, Sector 144, Noida, brings our neighbourhood together through devotion, ritual, culture and celebration. From Gautam Buddha Nagar, Uttar Pradesh, this digital heritage archive preserves those moments while helping visitors from every culture understand the meaning behind Bengal's Durga Puja traditions.
